Parent's guide to privacy

Version 2.1 · Effective 12 November 2026 · What changed

A short, practical guide for parents and guardians. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

In one minute

  • You are in charge. A child's account needs your consent. You can withdraw it and delete the account at any time.
  • Very little data: a first name, class, progress and study time. No photos, no location, no ID numbers.
  • No ads, no selling, no outside tracking. Analytics are internal, grouped numbers only.
  • Safe social features: friends only by mutual code, preset emoji cheers, no chat.
  • AI photo reading is off for under-18s.
  • Questions: support@knowtopia.in.

1. How your consent works

  1. When you create your child's account you confirm you are 18 or older and their parent or guardian, and you agree to the Terms and this privacy approach. We save the date, time and policy version you accepted.
  2. From 2027, Indian rules require a stronger check that a consenting adult is real. We may ask you to confirm by email, by a small verified payment, or through DigiLocker's age token. We will never ask for a copy of your Aadhaar.
  3. If your child's school created the account, the school asked for your consent. Contact the school first about school accounts, or write to us.

2. What your child's account holds

  • First name or nickname, avatar, board, class, subjects;
  • Progress: stars, XP, streaks, topics, quiz and test scores, puzzle times;
  • Study minutes per day (last 60 days), shown to you in Parent Hub;
  • Friends list and the emoji cheers received (cheers deleted after 30 days);
  • Plans and payments (made by an adult).

3. What others can see

  • Friends: first name, emoji avatar, streak, weekly stars and puzzle times. Never email, surname, school or location.
  • Live game rooms: players in the room see each other's name, avatar and score. Rooms need a code and are deleted after 2 hours.
  • Teachers: only for school-created accounts, only their own classes.

4. What you control in Parent Hub

  • Link your child with a one-time code (valid for 1 day).
  • Daily goal, daily time limit, quiet hours, break reminders, and a 4-digit PIN to unlock.
  • Exam planner and rewards.
  • Weekly summary email (switch off with one click in the email).
  • Controls: switch friends & cheers, live game rooms and friend news on or off for your child. Our server checks these switches, so they work on every phone your child uses.
  • Unlink at any time.

Prefer us to do it? Email support@knowtopia.in and we will switch friends or live games off the same working day.

5. AI and photos

The only AI feature that sends data to another company is handwriting reading for CA students (Google Gemini). It is for adults only and is switched off for anyone under 18. Children's answers and progress are never used to train AI.

6. Payments

Only an adult can pay. Payments are made on our website through Razorpay; we never see card or UPI details. Plans do not renew automatically. See Refunds.

7. See, download, correct or delete

Email support@knowtopia.in from your account email with the subject "Privacy request", and tell us what you want: a copy, a correction, or deletion. We reply within 15 days. Deleting removes your child's account and progress within 30 days (we keep only payment records the tax law needs).

You can also delete an account straight away in the app: Me → Your account → Delete my account (on your child's phone), or Parent Hub → Your account for your own account. See Delete your account.

8. A quick safety checklist

  • Use a nickname or first name only.
  • Talk to your child about only adding friends they know.
  • Set a daily limit and quiet hours that suit your family.
  • Keep the account password with you.
  • Report anything worrying to support@knowtopia.in. In an emergency call 112; for child help call 1098; for online crime call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
