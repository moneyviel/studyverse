Delete your account

Version 2.0 · Effective 9 November 2026 · What changed

You can delete your Knowtopia account and its data at any time: in the app, or by sending us an email.

The short version

  • In the app: Me (CA: Profile) → Your account → Delete my account. Parents: Parent Hub → Your account → Delete my account.
  • By email: write to support@knowtopia.in from the email you signed up with, subject "Delete my account".
  • Your progress, profile, friends and game results are deleted. Payment records are kept only as tax law requires.

1. Delete in the app

  1. Open Knowtopia and sign in.
  2. Students: tap 😊 Me (CA students: 👤 Profile). Parents: open Parent Hub.
  3. In the Your account card, tap Delete my account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, tap Continue, type DELETE and tap Delete my account forever.

The account is closed straight away and the app on that phone is cleared. This cannot be undone.

2. Delete by email (no app needed)

Send an email to support@knowtopia.in from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my account". If you are a parent asking for your child's account, write from your own (parent) account email and give the child's account email.

We may ask one question to make sure the request is really from you. We acknowledge within 24 hours and delete the account within 7 days (at the latest within 30 days, as our Privacy Policy says).

3. What is deleted and what is kept

What happens to each kind of data
DataWhat happens
Account (email, password), profile, name, avatar, class or courseDeleted
Learning progress, stars, streaks, study time, mistakes, notes, answersDeleted
Friends, friend code, cheers, weekly stars, game rooms and resultsDeleted
Links with a parent or children, parent goals and controlsDeleted (a parent's children keep their own accounts; they are only unlinked)
Records of what you agreed to (terms, consent)Deleted with the account
Payment and GST recordsKept for 8 years because tax law requires it, without your name or email linked to them
Safety reports you sent about othersKept for safety, without your name
BackupsOverwritten within 30 days
Photos of written answers and other things saved only on your phoneCleared from the phone you delete from. On other phones: sign out, or clear the app's data.

Same email in another MoneyViel app? If you also use another MoneyViel app (for example Salonzy) with the same email, we delete all your Knowtopia data but keep your login so that other app keeps working. Your data in that other app is not touched.

4. Children's and school accounts

A parent or guardian can delete their child's account: sign in on the child's phone and use Delete my account, or email us from the parent's account. For accounts made by a school or coaching institute, please ask the school first; we also act on a parent's request and tell the school.

5. Deleting only some data

To delete only part of your data (for example, your study history, or friends) without closing the account, email support@knowtopia.in. You can also remove friends yourself in Squad, and a parent can turn off friends and game rooms in Parent Hub → Controls.
