Intellectual Property & Copyright

Version 2.2 · Effective 13 November 2026 · What changed

Who owns Knowtopia content, how we refer to boards and textbooks, and how to tell us if you think something copies your work.

In short

  • Our lessons, questions, games, code and characters belong to us. Use them for your own learning.
  • We refer to CBSE, NCERT and ICAI syllabus names only to help you find lessons. We do not copy their books.
  • Think we copied your work? Email support@knowtopia.in and we act within 15 days.

1. Our content

The Knowtopia name and logo, app design, code, characters, illustrations, games, interactive labs, and our own explanations and questions are owned by or licensed to Mukesh Jhedu, and are protected by Indian law, including the Copyright Act, 1957 and the Trade Marks Act, 1999. You may use them for your own learning. You may not copy, publish, sell, share, scrape, or use them to make apps, books or AI training data without our written permission.

2. Other people's work we use

Knowtopia uses open-source software and free fonts under their licences, for example three.js (MIT licence, the three.js authors) for 3D labs, and the Baloo 2, Nunito and Noto Sans Devanagari fonts (SIL Open Font Licence) from Google Fonts. These remain the property of their owners.

3. Board, textbook and institute references

"CBSE", "NCERT", "ICAI", "The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India" and "CA" belong to their owners. We use these names only to describe the syllabus or course a lesson relates to. We do not use their logos and do not claim any link with or approval from them.

We do not reproduce NCERT textbooks or ICAI study material. We may mention chapter names, syllabus topics, standard definitions, formulas, accounting and legal provisions and facts, which are needed to teach the subject. Our explanations and questions are written for Knowtopia. Short quotations, if any, are for teaching and are credited.

4. Content made by users

What you create stays yours. Do not upload or use anything you do not have the right to use. We may remove content that seems to break someone's rights.

5. How to report copying (takedown notice)

Email our Grievance Officer at support@knowtopia.in with:

  1. your name, address, phone and email;
  2. a description of your work or mark, and proof that you own it or may act for the owner;
  3. where it appears in Knowtopia (class, subject, chapter, topic, or a screenshot);
  4. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not allowed;
  5. a statement that your notice is true; and
  6. your signature (a typed name is fine).

We acknowledge within 24 hours and aim to act within 15 days. We may disable the content while we check. We may share your notice with the person who provided the content.

6. If your content was removed

If something you shared was removed and you believe this was a mistake, write to the Grievance Officer explaining why. We will review it and tell you our decision.

7. Please be honest

Please do not send false notices. False claims may break the law.
