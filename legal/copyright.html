1. Our content

The StudyVerse name and logo, app design, code, characters, illustrations, games, interactive labs, and our own explanations and questions are owned by or licensed to Mukesh Jhedu, and are protected by Indian law, including the Copyright Act, 1957. You may use them for your own learning. You may not copy, publish, sell or share them, or make apps or books from them, without our written permission.

2. Other people's work we use

StudyVerse uses some open-source software and free fonts, used under their licences, for example:

  • three.js (for 3D labs), under the MIT licence, copyright the three.js authors;
  • Baloo 2, Nunito and Noto Sans Devanagari fonts, from Google Fonts, under the SIL Open Font Licence.

These remain the property of their owners.

3. Board and institute names and marks

"CBSE" and the Central Board of Secondary Education name and logo, "NCERT" and the National Council of Educational Research and Training name and logo, and "ICAI", "The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India" and the "CA" marks belong to their respective owners. We use these names only to describe the syllabus or course a lesson relates to. We do not use their logos, and we do not claim any link with or approval from them.

StudyVerse does not copy textbooks. We may mention chapter names and syllabus topics so students can find the right lesson. Our explanations and questions are written for StudyVerse. If you think we have used your material wrongly, please tell us (see below).

4. How to report copying (takedown notice)

If you believe something in StudyVerse copies your work or uses your trademark without permission, please email our Grievance Officer at [Grievance Officer email] with:

  1. your name, address, phone number and email;
  2. a description of your work or mark, and proof that you own it or are allowed to act for the owner;
  3. where it appears in StudyVerse (grade, subject, chapter, topic, or a screenshot);
  4. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not allowed by you or by law;
  5. a statement that the information in your notice is true; and
  6. your signature (a typed name is fine for email).

We will acknowledge your notice within 24 hours and aim to act on it within 15 days. Where needed, we may remove or disable the content while we look into it. We may share your notice with the person who provided the content.

5. If your content was removed

If something you shared was removed after a notice and you believe this was a mistake, you can write to the Grievance Officer explaining why, with your contact details. We will review it and tell you what we decide.

6. Please be honest

Please do not send false notices. Making false claims may break the law.
