Community Rules (Acceptable Use)

Last updated: 7 November 2026

These rules are for group study, squads, chat, shared answers and any other place where StudyVerse users can see each other's content. Some of these features are coming later. The rules apply as soon as they start.

On this page

  2. 2. Keep yourself and others safe
  3. 3. Not allowed
  4. 4. Reporting

1. Be kind and respectful

  • Help each other learn. Everyone learns at their own speed.
  • No bullying, teasing, threats, name-calling or leaving people out on purpose.
  • No hate or insults about anyone's religion, caste, race, gender, disability, language, region or looks.

2. Keep yourself and others safe

  • Do not share personal details: phone number, home address, school name, passwords, or photos of yourself or others.
  • Never agree to meet someone you only know online.
  • If anyone makes you feel uncomfortable or asks for personal details or photos, stop, tell a parent or teacher, and report it to us.
  • Adults must never try to contact children privately or inappropriately through StudyVerse.

3. Not allowed

Do not post or share anything that:

  • is sexual, obscene, violent or frightening, or harmful to children;
  • encourages self-harm, dangerous challenges, drugs or crime;
  • shares someone else's private information or pictures, or pretends to be someone else;
  • copies someone else's work without permission, or shares leaked exam papers or helps anyone cheat in a real exam;
  • is false and meant to mislead, or is spam, advertising, scams or links to unsafe sites;
  • contains viruses or harmful code; or
  • breaks any Indian law, or threatens the unity, security or public order of India.

4. Reporting

Use the report button (when available) or email our Grievance Officer at support@moneyviel.com. Tell us where it is and what happened. We will acknowledge your report within 24 hours. Reports about harmful content will be handled within the times shown on the Contact & Grievance page.

5. What we may do

If someone breaks these rules, we may remove the content, give a warning, limit or suspend access, or close the account. Where the law requires, or a child may be in danger, we may report it to the police or other authorities.

6. Need help now?

If you or someone else is in danger, call 112 (emergency). For help for a child in need, call the child helpline 1098. Talk to a parent, teacher or another adult you trust.
