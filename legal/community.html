Community Rules

Version 2.0 · Effective 9 November 2026 · What changed

StudyVerse has a few social features: friends, cheers, leaderboards and live game rooms. These rules keep them kind, fair and safe.

The rules in one line each

  • Be kind. Cheer people on. No bullying or teasing.
  • Be safe. Only add friends you know. Never share personal details.
  • Play fair. No cheating, bots or sharing answers.
  • Tell someone. If anything feels wrong, tell a grown-up and report it to us.

1. How our social features are built to be safe

  • Friends are added only with a private 6-letter friend code, and both people must accept. You can remove a friend at any time.
  • No chat, no photos, no links. Friends can only send preset cheers (👏 🔥 ⭐ 💪 👋 🚀). Cheers are deleted after 30 days.
  • Others see only a first name (up to 20 letters) and an emoji avatar, plus stars, streaks and puzzle times.
  • Live game rooms are joined with a room code and are deleted 2 hours after they end.
  • Parent accounts are not in friends or leaderboards. A linked parent can switch friends, live rooms and friend news off for their child in Parent Hub → Controls.

2. Be kind and respectful

  • Help each other learn. Everyone learns at their own speed.
  • No bullying, teasing, threats, or leaving people out on purpose.
  • No hate about anyone's religion, caste, gender, disability, language, region or looks.

3. Display names

Use your first name or a nickname. Display names must not include: a phone number, email, address, school name, social media handle, rude or hurtful words, or someone else's name. We may change a display name that breaks these rules.

4. Fair play

No bots, scripts, changed apps, extra accounts, sharing answers during a live game, or using bugs. Tell us about bugs instead. We may remove scores or limit access for cheating. See Terms, section 8.

5. Staying safe

  • Never share your password, phone, address, school or photos.
  • Never agree to meet someone you only know online.
  • Adults must never try to contact a child they do not know through StudyVerse.

6. Reporting a problem

If someone is unkind, uses a bad display name, cheats or makes you uncomfortable:

  1. Remove or block them: tap the friend in Squad → 🚫 Block (they can no longer send you requests or cheers), or leave the game room.
  2. Tell a parent or teacher.
  3. Report it in the app: tap the friend → 🚩 Report, or the 🚩 next to a player in a game room, and pick a reason. They are not told who reported.
  4. Or report it to our Grievance Officer at support@moneyviel.com: say who (display name or friend code), what happened and when. A screenshot helps.

We acknowledge reports within 24 hours and act on harmful content within 72 hours (within 24 hours for anything sexual or showing a person without consent).

7. What we may do

We may change or remove a display name, remove scores, remove friend links, warn, suspend or close an account. If a child may be in danger, or the law requires, we may tell the parent, the school, or the police.

8. Need help now?

Emergency: 112. Childline (free, for children): 1098. Online crime: 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in. Talk to a parent, teacher or another adult you trust.
