Schools & coaching: data terms

Version 2.1 · Effective 12 November 2026 · What changed

These terms apply when a school or coaching institute ("the school") uses Knowtopia for its students, including the free pilot. They sit alongside the school's order and our Terms of Use.

Who is responsible for what

  • The school decides to enrol its students and uploads their details. For those accounts, the school is the Data Fiduciary: it must have a lawful basis and parents' consent where needed.
  • We act as the school's Data Processor for those accounts: we use the data only to provide Knowtopia to the school, never for ads, and never sell it.
  • For parents' own Parent accounts, and for anyone who signs up directly, we are the Data Fiduciary.
  • When the agreement ends, we delete the school's student data within 30 days, or hand accounts to parents if the school and parents ask.

2. What the school promises

  • It has a lawful basis to share each student's data with us, and has given parents a clear notice and obtained verifiable parental consent where the DPDP Act requires it. The DPDP Rules give educational institutions some exemptions for educational activities and children's safety; the school should check with its own adviser how these apply to it.
  • It uploads only what is needed: name, class, section, roll number, and optional student and parent email. No Aadhaar or other ID numbers, dates of birth, addresses, photos, health, caste or religion.
  • It keeps the data accurate and tells us when a student leaves.
  • It gives dashboard access only to staff who need it, and removes staff who leave.
  • It passes on to us any parent's or student's request it cannot handle itself.

A sample notice the school can send to parents is in section 9.

3. What we promise the school

  • Use student data only to provide Knowtopia to the school and its students, as the school instructs, and as these terms describe.
  • No ads, no selling, no use for marketing to students, no sharing except with the service providers in our Privacy Policy, which follow the same promises.
  • Keep it confidential. Only the owner can access it, and only when needed for support, security or the law.
  • Keep it secure (encryption, access rules per school, class and teacher; logs kept for at least 1 year).
  • Help the school respond to parents' and students' requests (see, correct, delete) within the school's deadlines.
  • Tell the school of any personal data breach affecting its students within 24 hours of finding out, with what we know, so it can meet its own duties. We also report to the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
  • Tell the school before adding a new service provider that will handle its students' data.
  • We may use grouped, de-identified numbers (for example "students found Fractions hard") to improve lessons. These never identify a student or a school.

4. Who sees what

  • School admins: classes, staff and students of their school, seat usage, reports.
  • Teachers: progress, weak topics, homework and reports for their own classes only.
  • Parents (if linked): their own child only.
  • Students: their own progress; friends see only first name, avatar and stars (see Community Rules). The school can ask us to switch friends and live rooms off for its students.
  • Handwriting reading (Google Gemini) is switched off for school student accounts of children.

5. Bulk uploads and invitations

  • Rows with a student email: an account is created and the student (or parent) gets an email to set a password.
  • Rows without email: a single-use roll-number code is made for the school to hand out.
  • Parent emails: the parent gets an invitation to make a Parent account and is linked to the child when they sign in.
  • Limits: up to 500 rows and 100 emails per upload, 10 uploads an hour and 250 emails a day per school. We keep only a count of each upload (not a copy of the file).

6. End of the pilot or agreement

  • At the end, the school can download its class reports.
  • Within 30 days we delete the school's student data and teacher accounts, except: records we must keep by law (invoices), and accounts that a parent has chosen to keep as a family account (with the parent's own consent).
  • Pilot accounts end automatically after 30 days unless an order is placed.

7. Commercial summary

  • Price per student per month by class group (see the pricing page), billed for 10 months per academic year (April–March).
  • Minimum 30 students. Volume discounts: 100+ students 10%, 500+ 20%, 1000+ 30%.
  • Prices are + GST as applicable. A GST invoice is issued to the school's GSTIN.
  • Free 30-day pilot for one class.

8. Responsibility

Each side is responsible for its own breaches of these terms and of data protection law. Our overall limit is set out in the Limitation of Liability page and the school's order. Indian law applies, with courts at Pune.

9. Sample notice for parents (school can copy)

"Our school uses Knowtopia, a learning app run by MoneyViel (Pune), for practice in [classes]. To create your child's account we will share: your child's name, class, section and roll number, and (optional) your email. Knowtopia uses this only to provide the app to our school; it shows no ads and does not sell data. Teachers will see your child's progress in the app. You can ask the school or support@knowtopia.in to see, correct or delete your child's data, or to stop using the app. Details: knowtopia.in/legal/privacy.html. Please sign below / reply to give your consent."
