Accessibility Statement

Version 2.0 · Effective 9 November 2026 · What changed

We want every student to be able to learn with Knowtopia, including students who are blind or have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or have motor, reading or learning differences.

1. What Knowtopia offers

Open the Aa button at the top of the app for these settings:

  • Text size: five sizes. These legal pages follow the size you choose.
  • Listen mode: reads every screen and question aloud, numbers the choices, and lets you answer by voice, number keys or tapping.
  • Read aloud buttons on lessons, labs and game instructions, with a choice of voice and speed.
  • High contrast: stronger colours, thick outlines and ✓ / ✗ marks, so colour is not the only clue.
  • Reduce motion: stops bouncing, sliding and flashing animations.
  • Timers: normal, extra time, or no timer for timed games.
  • Vibration feedback, which can be turned off.
  • Screen readers and keyboards: buttons have names, pop-ups are announced as dialogs and close with Escape, and the app works with a keyboard.

High contrast and reduce motion also follow your phone's settings until you change them in the app.

2. Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, in line with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. We have not yet had an independent audit, so we do not claim full conformance.

3. Known gaps

  • Some 3D models, drag-and-drop labs and fast games are hard to use without sight or fine hand control. We describe them in words and read results aloud where we can.
  • Voice answers depend on your browser's speech recognition, which may not work in every browser, language or offline.
  • Some Hindi and Sanskrit content may not be read well by every device's voice.
  • Live game rooms have time limits that may be hard for some players. Practice mode has no timer.

4. Tell us what is hard

Email support@knowtopia.in with the screen or topic, what happened, and your device, browser or assistive technology. We will reply and try to fix it, or find another way to give you the content.
