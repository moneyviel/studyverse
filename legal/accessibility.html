Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 7 November 2026

We want every student to be able to learn with StudyVerse, including students who are blind or have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or have motor, reading or learning differences.

1. What StudyVerse offers

Open the Aa button at the top of the app for these settings:

  • Text size: five sizes, from Small to Huge. These legal pages follow the size you choose.
  • Listen mode: reads every screen and question aloud, numbers the choices, and lets you answer by voice, by number keys or by tapping.
  • Read aloud: 🔊 buttons on lessons, labs and game instructions, with a choice of voices and speed.
  • High contrast: stronger colours, thick outlines and ✓ / ✗ marks, so colour is not the only clue.
  • Reduce motion: stops bouncing, sliding and flashing animations.
  • Timers: normal, extra time, or no timer for timed games.
  • Vibration feedback for right and wrong answers, which can be turned off.
  • Screen readers and keyboards: buttons have names, pop-ups are announced as dialogs and can be closed with Escape, and the app can be used with a keyboard.

High contrast and reduce motion also follow your phone's own settings until you change them in the app.

2. Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. We have not yet had an independent accessibility audit, so we do not claim full conformance.

3. Known gaps

  • Some 3D models, drag-and-drop labs and fast games are hard to use without sight or fine hand control. We describe them in words and read results aloud where we can, and we are working to add more alternatives.
  • Voice answers depend on your browser's speech recognition, which may not work in every browser or language, or offline.
  • Some Hindi and Sanskrit content may not be read well by every device's voice.
  • Videos or audio added in future may not yet have captions.

4. Tell us what is hard

If anything in StudyVerse is hard for you to use, please email [Contact email] with the screen or topic and what happened, and which device, browser or assistive technology you use. We will reply and try to fix it, or find another way to give you the content.
