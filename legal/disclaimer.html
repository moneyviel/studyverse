1. Not linked to any board or exam body

Knowtopia is not linked to, approved by or endorsed by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), the National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT), the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), or any other board, university or exam body. We use their names only to say which syllabus or course a lesson helps with.

2. Not a coaching institute

Knowtopia helps students understand concepts. It is not a school, coaching centre or tutor, gives no certificate or qualification, and does not replace your school, teacher, textbook or the official study material for your course.

3. Content may contain mistakes

  • Lessons, questions, games and general knowledge may contain errors or be out of date. Syllabuses, exam patterns, laws and tax rates change.
  • Handwriting reading (AI) can misread your answer. Always check it.
  • A mark or "correct / wrong" in the app is a practice guide, not how an examiner will mark you.

For exams, follow official sources: NCERT textbooks, CBSE circulars, ICAI study material and announcements, and your teachers. If something differs, follow the official source, and please tell us.

4. No guarantee of marks or results

Knowtopia does not promise any marks, grade, rank, pass, admission or result.

5. Not professional advice

CA and commerce modules cover tax, law, accounting, audit, economics and finance for education only. They are not tax, legal, financial, investment or accounting advice and may not reflect the latest law. For real decisions, consult a qualified professional. Science and health topics are not medical advice.

6. Links to other websites

We may link to other websites (for example official board sites). We do not control them and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

7. Found a mistake?
