Policy change log

Version 2.2 · Effective 13 November 2026 · What changed

Every version of our legal pages, newest first. The version number and effective date also appear at the top of each page.

How we handle changes

  • Small changes (clearer wording, typos, new contact details): we update the page and list it here.
  • Important changes (new kind of data, new purpose, new service provider, new fees or rules that affect you): we tell you in the app and by email before they start, and ask you, or the parent of a child, to accept the new version. We record who accepted which version and when.

Version 2.2 · 13 November 2026

Optional phone reminders. They are off until a student switches them on, so you do not need to accept again.

  • Privacy §8, §11 and §13: students can choose one study reminder a day (around 5:30 pm, never at night or in school hours). We keep the phone's push address only to send them, and delete it when reminders are switched off. The phone's push service (for example Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) delivers them.
  • Parent's guide §4 and Privacy for kids: how reminders work, and the new Phone reminders switch in Parent Hub → Controls.

Version 2.1 · 12 November 2026

New sections in the Terms. No new kind of personal data is collected, so you do not need to accept again.

  • Terms §11 (new) — Mind Gym, Family Fun Zone and Parenting Compass: these are practice activities, not IQ, psychological or medical assessments. Parenting tips are general, not professional advice, and the page lists helplines (112, CHILDLINE 1098, Tele-MANAS 14416). Family Quiz Night uses only progress the parent can already see. Sneak peek shows only titles.
  • Terms §12 (new) — Our honest-selling promises: no guaranteed results, no fake reviews, the full price up front, no dark patterns, no loans or pressure selling, and no marketing to children (Consumer Protection Act 2019, E-Commerce Rules 2020, Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023, Coaching Advertisement Guidelines 2024).
  • Terms §19: added a clause on events outside our control (force majeure), with a fair extension or refund for paid plans.
  • The other Terms sections are renumbered (old §11–18 are now §13–20). The owner's and Grievance Officer's full name is shown on all pages.

Version 2.0 · 9 November 2026

All pages rewritten for accounts, paid plans, Parent Hub, friends and live games, and schools.

  • Privacy Policy: data listed for each kind of user; legal basis for each purpose; parental consent flow; what friends, parents and teachers see; handwriting reading for adults only; emails; owner analytics; full list of service providers (Supabase, Razorpay, Brevo, Google Gemini, GitHub Pages / Cloudflare, Google Fonts); retention periods; breach handling; DPDP Rules timeline.
  • New pages: Privacy for kids, Parent's guide to privacy, Schools & coaching data terms, and this change log.
  • Terms of Use: age rules and account types; free trial and free codes; one-time plans with no auto-renew, + GST; fair play in games; content ownership and syllabus references; schools; Google Play.
  • Refunds: plan table; 7-day refund on first purchase; wrong-class fix; school seats and pilot.
  • Community Rules: rewritten for friends, cheers, leaderboards and live rooms; display-name rules; reporting.
  • Contact & Grievance: Grievance Officer's full name, designation and phone; timelines for privacy requests.
  • Disclaimer, Liability, Copyright and Accessibility: updated for accounts and handwriting reading.
