Privacy Policy

Version 2.1 · Effective 12 November 2026 · What changed

This policy explains what personal data Knowtopia uses, why, who helps us run the service, how long we keep data, and the rights you have. Parents: there is a shorter Parent's guide, and children can read Privacy for kids.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to teach, save progress and run your plan: an email, a first name or nickname, class and course, and your learning progress.
  • Children (under 18): a parent or guardian creates the account and gives consent. A school can also create accounts for its own students.
  • No ads. No selling of data. We do not track children across other apps or sites, we do not build profiles for advertising, and we never show targeted ads.
  • Friends see very little: only a first name, an avatar emoji and game stars. There is no chat and no photo sharing.
  • Handwriting reading (sends a photo to Google Gemini) is only for adult CA learners. It is switched off for anyone under 18.
  • Your data is stored in Mumbai, India (Supabase). A few helpers (payments, email, hosting) are listed in section 11.
  • You can see, correct, download or delete your data. Write to our Grievance Officer at support@knowtopia.in.

2. Words we use

  • Child: anyone under 18, as the DPDP Act says.
  • Parent: a parent or lawful guardian of a child.
  • Personal data: information about a person who can be identified from it.
  • Data Fiduciary: the one who decides why and how personal data is used. Data Processor: the one who handles data for a Data Fiduciary, following its instructions.
  • Data Principal: the person the data is about. For a child, the parent acts for them.

5. Children and parental consent

Most Knowtopia learners are children. The DPDP Act says we must get verifiable consent from a parent before we process a child's personal data, and that we must not track children, monitor their behaviour, or target ads at them. We follow this.

How a child gets an account

  1. A parent creates it: the adult signs up, confirms they are 18 or older and the child's parent or guardian, reads this policy and the Terms, and gives consent. We record who consented, when and to which version.
  2. Or a school creates it: the school enrols its own students and is responsible for getting parents' consent (see Schools).
  3. Linking a parent: a parent can link to the child in Parent Hub with a one-time code that expires in 1 day. A linked parent can see the child's progress, set limits and withdraw consent.

Checking that the adult is real

The DPDP Rules, 2025 ask us to check that the person giving consent is an identifiable adult, for example using details already known to us, a verified email, a payment made by the adult, or an age-and-identity token from an authorised service such as DigiLocker. We are adding these checks before the Rules apply on 13 May 2027 (see section 18). If we cannot confirm consent, we may limit or close the child's account.

What we never do with a child's data

  • No advertising of any kind, and no targeted or behavioural ads.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no advertising IDs.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No handwriting / AI photo reading for anyone under 18.
  • No free-text chat, no photo sharing, and no showing of a child's email, surname, school or location to other users.
  • No marketing emails to children.

Inside Knowtopia, we do record progress (for example, which topics are done and how long was studied). That is the learning service itself. It is shown to the child, to a linked parent and, for school accounts, to the child's teacher. It is never used for advertising or sold.

If you think a child's data reached us without a parent's consent, tell the Grievance Officer and we will delete it.

The same care applies to a person with a disability who has a lawful guardian: the guardian gives consent for them.

7. Handwriting reading (adult CA learners only)

CA learners can photograph a handwritten answer so the app can read it and help mark it. When you use this:

  • the photo goes from your phone, through our server function, to Google Gemini (Google's AI), which turns the handwriting into text. You check the text before it is marked;
  • we do not store the photo on our servers. We keep only short technical logs (time, success or error), not the photo or its text;
  • we currently use Google's free service level. Google's terms say it may use what it receives to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. So do not write personal details (name, roll number, phone) on the answer sheet;
  • Google may process the photo outside India;
  • this feature is only for adults (18+) and is switched off for anyone under 18 and for school-managed child accounts.

Using it is your choice. You can always type or self-mark instead.

8. Emails we send

  • Account emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset, school invitations, receipts): needed to run your account.
  • Weekly parent summary: one short email a week to linked parents. Every email has a "Stop weekly emails" link, and you can switch it back on in Parent Hub.
  • Important notices: changes to these policies, a security problem, or a plan ending.

Emails are sent by Brevo for us (section 11). We do not send marketing emails to children. We will only send offers to adults who have agreed, with an easy way to stop.

9. Improving the service (owner analytics)

The owner of Knowtopia has a private dashboard that shows overall numbers: for example, how many people signed up, how many are active, which topics are popular, which questions many people get wrong, and how many plans were bought. We use it only to fix mistakes and make lessons better.

  • It is built from our own database. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook pixels, advertising SDKs or any third-party tracking tool.
  • Only the owner can see it. It is never shared or sold.
  • We look at children's data only as grouped numbers for this purpose. We do not watch or profile an individual child, except when needed to answer a support request or a complaint about that account, or to stop misuse.

10. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising networks or advertising IDs.
  • We do not share data with anyone except the service providers in section 11, a linked parent, your school (for school accounts), or when the law requires it.
  • We do not use your data to train our own or anyone's AI models.
  • We do not knowingly do anything with a child's data that could harm the child's well-being.

14. Keeping data safe, and data breaches

  • Data is encrypted while it travels (HTTPS) and stored by Supabase with encryption at rest.
  • Database access rules let each account see only its own data (and a linked parent or teacher only what is allowed).
  • Passwords are stored as secure hashes. Secret keys stay on the server.
  • Only the owner can reach the admin tools, with strong sign-in.
  • We keep access logs to spot misuse.

If there is a personal data breach, we will tell affected users (or the parent, for a child) without delay, in plain words: what happened, what it means for you, what we are doing and what you can do. We will tell the Data Protection Board of India without delay and send it a full report within 72 hours, and report to CERT-In within 6 hours where its rules require. For school accounts we will also tell the school at once.

Please use a screen lock on your device and a strong password that you do not use elsewhere.

15. Cookies and storage on your device

Knowtopia does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's storage only to make the app work:

  • Sign-in: a sign-in token, so you stay signed in;
  • Settings: text size, high contrast, reduce motion, sound, voice and timers;
  • Progress copy: a copy of your progress so the app works offline, then syncs to your account;
  • Notices: whether you have seen a notice;
  • Offline copy of the app (a "service worker"), which holds the app's files, not your personal data.

To remove them, sign out and clear this site's data in your browser settings.

16. Your rights, and how to use them

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Know what personal data we have about you and who we shared it with;
  • Correct, complete or update it;
  • Erase it (unless a law makes us keep something, like tax records). You can delete your whole account yourself in the app (Me → Your account → Delete my account) or by email: see Delete your account;
  • Get a copy (we will send a file of your account data);
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. This stops future use. Things done before stay lawful. Some features may then stop working;
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India;
  • Nominate a person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act.

A parent uses these rights for their child. For school accounts, the school handles these requests first and we help it (you can also write to us and we will pass it on).

How: email support@knowtopia.in from the account's email, with the subject "Privacy request". We may ask a question to confirm it is you. We will acknowledge within 24 hours and reply within 15 days (at the latest within 30 days if the request is complex, and we will tell you why).

The law also asks you to give true information, not to pretend to be someone else, and not to make false or frivolous complaints.

18. The laws we follow

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025. The Rules were notified on 13 November 2025. Rules on the Board started at once; consent-manager rules start on 13 November 2026; the main duties (notice, consent, children's data, security, breach reporting, rights) apply from 13 May 2027. We already follow them where we can.
  • Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Sensitive Personal Data) Rules, 2011 (until replaced by the DPDP Act) and the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.
  • Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, for paid plans.
  • When Knowtopia is on Google Play, we will also follow Google Play's Families and User Data policies.
