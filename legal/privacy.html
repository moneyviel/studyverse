Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information StudyVerse uses, where it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. We have tried to write it so a parent can read it in a few minutes.

The short version

  • An account is needed to use StudyVerse. It is created with an email address and a password. A child's account must be created by a parent or guardian.
  • Your progress is saved to your account (on secure servers in Mumbai, India) so it is safe and works on any phone. Settings such as text size stay on your device.
  • No advertising. No tracking. We do not track children, watch their behaviour for ads, or show targeted ads to children.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We collect only what is needed to run your account: your email, your progress and the courses you can use.

2. What is saved on your device today

StudyVerse saves the following in your browser's storage (called "local storage") on your own phone, tablet or computer. It stays there. We do not receive it.

Information saved on your device
WhatWhy
Profile: the first name or nickname you type, your chosen character, board, grade, stream and subjectsTo greet you and show the right lessons

StudyVerse also keeps a copy of its own pages and files on your device (using a "service worker") so it works offline. This copy contains the app, not your personal data.

Tip: You do not need to type your real full name. A first name or nickname is enough.

3. What can leave your device

A few things happen whenever any website or app is used:

  • Loading the app. When your device downloads StudyVerse, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.)) receives normal technical details such as your IP address, browser type, the files asked for and the time. This is needed to deliver the app and keep it secure. We do not use it to identify or profile you. Such logs are kept for 90 days.
  • Fonts. StudyVerse uses free fonts from Google Fonts. To load them, your device connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. Google's own privacy policy applies to that.
  • Speaking your answers (optional). If you use the microphone (for example in Listen mode or "Ask"), your browser turns your speech into text. Some browsers, such as Google Chrome, send the audio to the browser maker's servers to do this. StudyVerse does not receive or keep the recording. You can always tap or type instead.
  • Read-aloud. Read-aloud uses the voices on your device or browser. Some browsers offer online voices run by the browser maker.
  • Sharing. If you tap "Share" (for example to challenge a friend), you choose the app to share with (such as WhatsApp). Only the message shown is shared, and that app's privacy policy applies.

4. Cookies and local storage

StudyVerse does not set advertising or analytics cookies. We use local storage and the offline copy described above only to make the app work and remember your settings.

To delete everything StudyVerse saved on a device: go to Me → Start over (this erases progress), or clear this site's data in your browser settings. This cannot be undone.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising networks.
  • We do not track children, monitor their behaviour, or target advertising at them.
  • We do not use analytics tools that build a profile of you.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not knowingly process data in a way that could harm a child's well-being.

6. Children and parental consent

Under the DPDP Act, anyone under 18 is a "child". Most StudyVerse users are children, so we take extra care.

  • Accounts for children are created by a parent or guardian. When creating an account, the adult confirms that they are 18 or older and, if the account is for a child, that they are the child's parent or lawful guardian and give consent for the child's progress to be kept as this policy describes.
  • As the DPDP Act (Section 9) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 come into force, we will add any further check of the adult's identity that the law requires.
  • A parent or guardian can see, correct or delete their child's data, and withdraw consent, at any time.
  • We will never track a child, monitor a child's behaviour, or show targeted advertising to a child.
  • The same care applies to a person with a disability who has a lawful guardian.

If you believe a child has given us personal data without a parent's consent, please contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete it.

7. Accounts: what we keep on our servers

When you create an account, we keep:

  • Account details: the email address and a securely scrambled (hashed) password. We never see your password;
  • Learning data: the progress saved in the app (for example name or nickname, grade, board, stars, results and answers you checked), so it can be saved and synced across devices;
  • Course access: which courses or grades your account can use, and any code you used;
  • Payment records: what was bought, when, the amount and any discount code. Card, UPI or bank details are handled by Razorpay, our payment company, not stored by us;
  • Photo answers you choose to have read: the photos are sent to Google Gemini only to turn the handwriting into text, which you then check before marking. We do not keep the photos on our servers (they stay on your phone). Google's free service may use what it receives to improve its products, so do not write personal details on answer sheets;
  • Support messages you send us.

We use this data only to run StudyVerse, keep it safe, give you support, handle payments and follow the law. We keep it while your account is open, and delete it when you ask us to close the account, except what a law requires us to keep (for example, tax records for payments).

Service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in; data stored in Mumbai, India), GitHub Pages (delivers the app), Razorpay (payments) and Google Gemini (reading handwriting in photo answers, only when you ask for it). They may only use the data to provide their service to us. If any data is ever stored or processed outside India, we will tell you and follow Indian law on it.

8. Keeping data safe

Data on your device is protected by your device. Please use a screen lock. Data in your account is protected with encryption in transit, secure password storage and access rules that let each account see only its own data. If there is a personal data breach, we will inform affected users and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • know what personal data we have about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • have it erased, unless a law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (things already done before you withdrew stay lawful);
  • complain to us and get a reply (see section 11);
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or are unable to.

A parent or guardian can use these rights for their child. You can see your progress in the app (Me screen). To get a copy, correct it, or close your account and delete its data, contact our Grievance Officer (section 11).

The DPDP Act also asks users to give true information and not to make false or frivolous complaints.

10. India's data protection law

The DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 are being brought into force in stages, with most duties scheduled to apply from 2027. StudyVerse is built to collect as little as possible, and we will follow these laws, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, as they apply to us.
