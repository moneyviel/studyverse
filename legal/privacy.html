Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information StudyVerse uses, where it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. We have tried to write it so a parent can read it in a few minutes.

The short version

  • No sign-up today. We do not collect your name, progress or answers on our servers.
  • What you enter (first name, grade, progress, settings) is saved only in the browser on your device.
  • No advertising. No tracking. We do not track children, watch their behaviour for ads, or show targeted ads to children.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • When sign-up starts, we will update this policy first and ask a parent's or guardian's consent before collecting any data about a child.

2. What is saved on your device today

StudyVerse saves the following in your browser's storage (called "local storage") on your own phone, tablet or computer. It stays there. We do not receive it.

Information saved on your device

Profile: the first name or nickname you type, your chosen character, board, grade, stream and subjectsTo greet you and show the right lessons

Tip: You do not need to type your real full name. A first name or nickname is enough.

3. What can leave your device

Even without accounts, a few things happen whenever any website or app is used:

  • Loading the app. When your device downloads StudyVerse, our hosting provider ([Hosting provider name]) receives normal technical details such as your IP address, browser type, the files asked for and the time. This is needed to deliver the app and keep it secure. We do not use it to identify or profile you. Such logs are kept for [log retention period].
  • Fonts. StudyVerse uses free fonts from Google Fonts. To load them, your device connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. Google's own privacy policy applies to that.
  • Speaking your answers (optional). If you use the microphone (for example in Listen mode or "Ask"), your browser turns your speech into text. Some browsers, such as Google Chrome, send the audio to the browser maker's servers to do this. StudyVerse does not receive or keep the recording. You can always tap or type instead.
  • Read-aloud. Read-aloud uses the voices on your device or browser. Some browsers offer online voices run by the browser maker.
  • Sharing. If you tap "Share" (for example to challenge a friend), you choose the app to share with (such as WhatsApp). Only the message shown is shared, and that app's privacy policy applies.

4. Cookies and local storage

StudyVerse does not set advertising or analytics cookies. We use local storage and the offline copy described above only to make the app work and remember your settings.

To delete everything StudyVerse saved on a device: go to Me → Start over (this erases progress), or clear this site's data in your browser settings. This cannot be undone.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising networks.
  • We do not track children, monitor their behaviour, or target advertising at them.
  • We do not use analytics tools that build a profile of you.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not knowingly process data in a way that could harm a child's well-being.

6. Children and parental consent

Under the DPDP Act, anyone under 18 is a "child". Most StudyVerse users are children, so we take extra care.

  • Today: we do not collect any child's personal data on our servers. Everything stays on the device.
  • When sign-up starts: before we collect or use any personal data of a child, we will get verifiable consent from the child's parent or lawful guardian, as the DPDP Act (Section 9) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 require. We will check that the person giving consent is an adult, using a method the law allows.
  • A parent or guardian can see, correct or delete their child's data, and withdraw consent, at any time.
  • We will never track a child, monitor a child's behaviour, or show targeted advertising to a child.
  • The same care applies to a person with a disability who has a lawful guardian.

If you believe a child has given us personal data without a parent's consent, please contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete it.

7. When accounts and paid plans start

We plan to add sign-up for students and parents, paid plans, and optional AI answer checking. Before any of these start, we will update this policy, show you a clear notice of exactly what we collect and why, and ask for consent where the law needs it. In general we expect to need:

  • Account details: a name or nickname, a parent's email address or mobile number, the child's grade and board, and login details;
  • Learning data: progress and results, so they can be saved and synced across devices;
  • Payment records (for paid plans): what was bought, when, and the amount. Card or bank details will be handled by the payment company, not stored by us;
  • AI answer checking (optional): the answers you choose to send, so they can be checked;
  • Support messages you send us.

We will use such data only to run StudyVerse, keep it safe, give you support, handle payments and follow the law. We will keep it only as long as it is needed for that, or as long as a law requires (for example, tax records for payments), and then delete it. We may use trusted service providers (for example, hosting, database, email, payment and AI providers) who may only use the data to provide their service to us. We will name the main ones in this policy. If any data is stored or processed outside India, we will tell you and follow Indian law on it.

8. Keeping data safe

Data on your device is protected by your device. Please use a screen lock. When we start keeping data on our servers, we will use reasonable security safeguards, such as encryption and access controls. If there is a personal data breach, we will inform affected users and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • know what personal data we have about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • have it erased, unless a law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (things already done before you withdrew stay lawful);
  • complain to us and get a reply (see section 11);
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or are unable to.

A parent or guardian can use these rights for their child. Today, because your data is only on your device, you can see, change and delete it yourself in the app (Me screen) or in your browser settings.

The DPDP Act also asks users to give true information and not to make false or frivolous complaints.

10. India's data protection law

The DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 are being brought into force in stages, with most duties scheduled to apply from 2027. StudyVerse is built to collect as little as possible, and we will follow these laws, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, as they apply to us.
