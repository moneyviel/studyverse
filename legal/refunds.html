Refund & Cancellation Policy

Version 2.0 · Effective 9 November 2026 · What changed

This policy explains cancellations and refunds for StudyVerse plans. It is part of our Terms of Use.

In short

  • No auto-renew. Every plan is paid once, so there is nothing to cancel to stop future charges.
  • Changed your mind? Full refund if you ask within 7 days of your first purchase.
  • Charged twice, or paid but the plan did not start? Full refund, any time.
  • Refunds reach your original payment method within 7 working days of approval.
  • Prices are + GST as applicable. GST is refunded along with the price.

2. Before you pay

Only an adult (18+) may pay. Before you pay, the pricing and payment pages show the plan, class or level, length, price, GST and the final amount. Please check you chose the right class or CA level.

3. Free trial and free codes

The 24-hour free trial needs no payment and ends by itself. Free codes are free, so there is nothing to refund.

4. Cancelling

  • Plans do not renew, so you never need to cancel to avoid a future charge.
  • If you want to stop a plan early and get money back, see section 5.
  • We charge no cancellation fee.

5. Refunds for School and CA plans

You get a full refund (price + GST) if:

  • you ask within 7 days of your first purchase of a School or CA plan on the account, for any reason (later purchases: see the special-reason rule below);
  • you were charged twice or charged by mistake;
  • you paid but the plan was not added to the account, and we could not fix it within 2 working days; or
  • the plan was seriously faulty or not as described, and we could not fix it.

Wrong class or level? Write within 7 days and we will move the plan to the right class or level (paying or refunding any price difference).

After 7 days, a part-used plan is not usually refunded. If you have a special reason (for example illness), write to us and we will look at it fairly. If we stop offering StudyVerse or close your account without your fault, we refund the unused part.

6. Schools and coaching institutes

  • The free 30-day pilot costs nothing and does not turn into a paid order by itself.
  • Seat orders follow the signed order or invoice. If an order is cancelled before student accounts are activated, we refund it in full.
  • After activation, a school may reduce seats for whole months not yet started, with 30 days' notice; we refund or credit those months. Seats already used are not refunded.

7. How to ask for a refund

Email support@moneyviel.com with the account email, payment date, amount and Razorpay payment ID (in your receipt), and the reason. We acknowledge within 24 hours and decide within 7 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days; your bank may take a few more days under RBI rules. A GST credit note is issued where needed.

8. Chargebacks

Please talk to us before asking your bank to reverse a payment. We will usually solve it faster. If a chargeback is made, the plan may be paused until it is settled.

9. If you are not happy

Contact our Grievance Officer (see Contact & Grievance). You also have rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019: you can call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915 or file a complaint at e-daakhil.nic.in.
