1. Who can pay

Only an adult (18 or over), such as a parent or guardian, may buy a plan. Children should ask a parent or guardian.

2. Before you pay

Before you pay, we will clearly show the price (including taxes), what the plan includes, how long it lasts, whether it renews automatically, and how to cancel.

3. Cancelling

  • You can cancel a plan at any time from [where in the app] or by emailing [Contact email].
  • If your plan renews automatically, cancelling stops the next payment. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • We will not charge a cancellation fee, unless the same kind of charge would also apply to us if we cancelled your order.
  • We will remind you [number of days] days before an automatic renewal.

4. Refunds

You can get a full refund if:

  • you ask within [number of days] days of your first purchase of a plan;
  • you were charged twice, or charged by mistake;
  • money was taken but the plan was not given to you; or
  • the paid service was seriously faulty or was not as described, and we could not fix it.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within [number of working days] working days of approval. Your bank or payment provider may take some extra time, following Reserve Bank of India rules.

Apart from these cases, a part-used period is usually not refunded, but please write to us if you have a special reason and we will consider it fairly.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email [Contact email] with the account email or phone, date of payment, amount, payment reference, and the reason. We will acknowledge your request within 24 hours and resolve it within one month at most.

6. If you are not happy

Contact our Grievance Officer (see Contact & Grievance). You also have rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and can contact the National Consumer Helpline (1915).
