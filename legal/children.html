Privacy for kids

Version 2.0 · Effective 9 November 2026 · What changed

Hi! This page is for you. It explains, in simple words, what StudyVerse keeps about you and how we keep it safe. Read it with your mum, dad or the grown-up who looks after you.

The 5 big things

  1. A grown-up (your parent or your school) made your account and said yes to it.
  2. We keep your first name, class and progress (like stars and topics done) so you never lose your work.
  3. Friends only see your first name, your emoji face and your stars. Nothing else.
  4. No ads, ever. We never sell anything about you.
  5. Anything feels wrong? Tell a grown-up you trust.

What does StudyVerse keep about me?

We keep:

  • your first name or nickname and your character or emoji,
  • your class and subjects,
  • your progress: stars, streaks, quizzes, games and how long you studied.

We keep these so you can carry on from where you stopped, on any phone.

We do not want your full name, home address, phone number, photos or where you live. Please never type them in the app.

Who can see my things?

  • You can see all your progress.
  • Your parent (if they linked to you) can see your progress and study time, to cheer you on.
  • Your teacher can see your progress, if your school gave you the account.
  • Friends you said yes to can see only your first name, emoji face, stars and streak.
  • In a live game, other players see your name, emoji and score for that game.

How we keep things safe and friendly

  • There is no chat in StudyVerse. Friends can only send happy emoji cheers like 👏 and 🔥.
  • You can only add a friend if you both say yes, using a secret friend code.
  • No ads, and nobody follows what you do on other apps.
  • Your account is locked with a password. Do not share it, except with your parent.

Your safety tips

  • Only add friends you know in real life.
  • Never share your password, phone number, address or school with anyone online.
  • If something makes you sad, scared or uncomfortable, stop and tell a grown-up. They can tell us and we will help.
  • If you are in danger, call 112. For a helper who listens to children, call 1098 (free).

You are the boss of your information

You and your parent can ask us to:

  • show what we keep,
  • fix something that is wrong,
  • delete your account and everything in it.

Ask your parent to email support@moneyviel.com. The grown-up version of this page is the Privacy Policy, and there is a guide for parents.
