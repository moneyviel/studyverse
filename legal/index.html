Legal & Privacy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

StudyVerse is a learning app run by Mukesh Jhedu, an individual in India. These pages explain the rules, your privacy and what you can expect from us, in simple words.

In short

  • You need an account to use StudyVerse (a parent or guardian creates a child's account). Your progress is saved to your account so it is safe on any phone.
  • No ads, and no tracking of children.
  • StudyVerse helps you understand topics. It is not a coaching institute and is not linked to CBSE, NCERT or ICAI.
  • Our content can have mistakes. For exams, always check your official textbook and the board's own website.
  • Parents: if your child is under 18, please read the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy with them.
