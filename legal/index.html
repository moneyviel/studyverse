The rules, your privacy and what you can expect from us, in simple words. Knowtopia is run by Mukesh J (MoneyViel) in Pune, India.

In short

  • A parent or guardian (or a school) creates a child's account and gives consent.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no tracking of children across other apps.
  • Knowtopia helps you understand topics. It is not linked to CBSE, NCERT or ICAI, and can make mistakes.
  • Plans are paid once on our website, no auto-renew, + GST as applicable, with a 7-day refund on first purchase.
  • Questions or complaints: support@knowtopia.in.
