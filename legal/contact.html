3. How to complain

Please include:

  • your name and how to contact you (if the user is a child, a parent or guardian should write);
  • what the problem is, and where in the app (grade, subject, topic, screen, or a screenshot);
  • for payments: the date, amount and payment reference;
  • what you would like us to do.

We will give you a complaint reference number.

4. How quickly we reply

Type of complaintWe acknowledge withinWe aim to resolve within
Any complaint (content, privacy, safety, copyright, accessibility)24 hours15 days
Request to remove harmful or unlawful content shared by users (when sharing features start)24 hours72 hours
Complaints about paid plans or refunds (when payments start)24 hours1 month at most

These timelines follow the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, where they apply. Where the law requires faster action for a particular kind of complaint, we will act faster.

5. If you are not satisfied

  • Content decisions: you may appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee set up by the Government of India, within 30 days of our decision.
  • Privacy: you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India after using our grievance process.
  • Paid services: you can contact the National Consumer Helpline (1915) or approach a consumer commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

6. Governing law

Our Terms of Use are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Pune have jurisdiction, without affecting your consumer rights.
