3. How to complain

Please write from your account email and include:

  • your name and how to contact you (if the user is a child, a parent should write);
  • what happened, where in the app, and when (a screenshot helps);
  • for payments: date, amount and Razorpay payment ID;
  • what you would like us to do.

We will give you a complaint reference number.

5. If you are not satisfied

  • Content decisions: you may appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee of the Government of India (gac.gov.in) within 30 days of our decision.
  • Privacy: after using our process, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Paid plans: National Consumer Helpline 1915, or a consumer commission (e-daakhil.nic.in).
  • Online crime: call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
