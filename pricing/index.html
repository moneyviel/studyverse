Questions parents and students ask

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you ask within 7 days of your first purchase, we give a full refund. The money goes back to your original payment method within 7 working days. Read our Refund policy.

Will my plan renew automatically?

No auto-renew: we remind you before it ends. You pay again only if you want to continue.

Can I switch to a different class?

Each School plan is for one class only. After the 24-hour free trial, the app shows only the class you paid for. Moving up to a new class next year? Buy a plan for the new class. Chose the wrong class by mistake? Write to support@moneyviel.com within 7 days and we'll fix it. CA Foundation is a separate plan.

I am a parent. Can I pay for my child?

Yes. Create a Parent account, link your child in the app (Parent Home), then sign in here and choose your child under “Who is this plan for?”. The plan goes on your child’s account.

What happens when the plan ends?

Nothing is lost. All progress stays saved, and you go back to the free plan (5 questions a day) until you renew.

I need help

Write to support@moneyviel.com. We usually reply within a day.
