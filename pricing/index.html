Questions parents and students ask

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you ask within 7 days of your first purchase, we give a full refund. The money goes back to your original payment method within 7 working days. Read our Refund policy.

Will my plan renew automatically?

No auto-renew. Every plan is a one-time payment; we remind you before it ends. You pay again only if you want to continue.

Is GST included?

No. Prices shown exclude GST; GST is added as applicable when you pay. The payment page shows the final amount before you pay.

Why is the CA plan 4 months?

4 months is about one exam attempt season — enough to finish the syllabus, revise and take mock papers before your exam. You can also pay month by month.

Can I switch to a different class?

Each School plan is for one class only; the price depends on the class (Class 1–5, 6–8, 9–10, 11–12). After the 24-hour free trial, the app shows only the class you paid for. Moving up to a new class next year? Buy a plan for the new class. Chose the wrong class by mistake? Write to support@knowtopia.in within 7 days and we'll fix it. CA plans are separate.

I am a parent. Can I pay for my child?

Yes. Create a Parent account, link your child in the app (Parent Home), then sign in here and choose your child under “Who is this plan for?”. The plan goes on your child’s account.

What happens when the plan ends?

Nothing is lost. All progress stays saved, and you go back to the free plan (5 questions a day) until you renew.

Do you have plans for schools or coaching institutes?

Yes — from ₹100 per student per month (Class 1–5) to ₹225 (Class 11–12), and ₹250 for CA Foundation coaching, billed for 10 months a year (+ GST as applicable). 10–30% off for 100+ students, minimum 30 students, and a free 30-day pilot for one class. Teachers get a class dashboard and bulk student accounts. See “For schools & coaching institutes” above or write to support@knowtopia.in.
